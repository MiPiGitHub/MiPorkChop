Add to Home Screen

iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app if offered.

An internet connection is required for current status.

How check-in works

Optional estimate, one person per browser. On the way expires after 15 minutes; check-in after 6 hours. Closing clears both. PorkChop is counted automatically while open.

Use the same browser to check out. Clearing cookies or switching browsers may require entering your nickname again. No GPS.

Status details

Updates are checked every 15 seconds. If the garage hasn’t reported in over 7½ minutes, its status is unavailable.
